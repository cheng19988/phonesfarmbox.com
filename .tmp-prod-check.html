FAQ

Can I mount my own phones?+

Yes. Send model list and dimensions — we confirm slot compatibility and power draw before quote.

USB vs OTG vs hybrid connection — which do I need?+

USB mode fits standard wired debugging and host-side control. OTG mode applies when your quoted ROM or control path needs OTG-style wiring. Hybrid is confirmed during quotation when you combine different phone models, ROM paths, or host workflows — we do not assume all ROMs support every mode.

What voltage plug do I get?+

110V, 220V, or 220–240V regional option confirmed before quote. Final PSU, cable set, and plug standard depend on destination country and chassis configuration — wattage is sized from your BOM, not fixed on this page.

Is SIM supported on every model?+

No — SIM path depends on phone model and ROM. We mark SIM-capable models on the proforma.

How many PCs do I need?+

Most buyers run 1 PC per 1–3 boxes depending on hub tier and script load — confirm at quote.

Can boxes stack in a rack?+

Yes — stackable chassis with airflow spacing noted in packing guide.

Can I get packing photos before shipment?+

Yes — request on RFQ or quote reply. Photos and shipping size provided after packing plan is confirmed when available for your build.

What is not included?+

Phones (unless quoted), host PC, proxies/VPN, and destination import duties.

Does this include phones?+

Not by default — phones are line items on quote when you choose phone-included or turnkey device sourcing. Empty chassis ships without devices unless on BOM.

What should I prepare before quote?+

Destination country, quantity, model list, connection mode, voltage region, and whether you need packing photos or datasheet — same fields as our contact RFQ form.

Does remote setup include account operation?+

No — remote setup covers host PC connectivity, USB wiring, and batch-control verification. It does not include operating your social media accounts, proxies, or platform outcome guarantees.
